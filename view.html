Connect your Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar settings.
  2. In the left sidebar under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
  4. Paste it below. It's stored only in this browser.

Treat this link like a password: anyone who has it can see your calendar.
